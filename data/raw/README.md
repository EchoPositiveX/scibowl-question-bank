# Raw PDFs

National Science Bowl sample questions published by the U.S. Department of Energy, copied unchanged from the old `nsb` repo.

Layout: `data/raw/{level}/{set}/{round}.pdf`, for example `data/raw/ms/set01/m_round01.pdf`.

| Level | Sets | PDFs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ms (middle school) | set01–set15 | 241 |

Known oddities:

- `ms/set13` has four extra rounds with special names (`de1_for_web`, `de3_for_web`, `rr2_for_web`, `rr5_for_web`).
- `ms/set15/Set-7-MS-2021.pdf` replaced a high school round (`Set-7-HS-2021.pdf`) that had been copied into the middle school set by mistake.
